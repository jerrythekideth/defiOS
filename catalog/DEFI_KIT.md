## DeFi_os cards mapped to defi-kit

* **lido** ← `lido/deposit`: one bundle: stETH/wstETH approvals to wstETH and unstETH, submit (send), wrap, unwrap, requestWithdrawals[WstETH](_owner = avatar), claimWithdrawal(s)
* **gearbox_v3** ← `gearbox/deposit`: lending pools only (approve asset, deposit/depositWithReferral, withdraw/redeem pinned to the avatar). Credit accounts (open/multicall/close) are not in defi-kit.
* **morpho_vaults** ← `morphoVaults/deposit`: approve asset + ERC-4626 deposit/withdraw/redeem pinned to the avatar, per listed vault
* **cowswap** ← `cowswap/swap`: sell/buy token sets, receiver = avatar, feeAmountBP <= feeAmountBp (default 0), optional appData pin; sell=ETH also allows WETH.deposit (weth.wrap). twap=true adds ComposableCoW (not carded).
